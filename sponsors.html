Why Partner With Us

More Than a Logo Placement.

We competed in our first-ever Not a Boring Competition in 2026 — and won Rookie of the Year against teams from around the world. Our machine was designed, built, and operated entirely by students from the Grainger College of Engineering at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.

Sponsoring the Boring Illini puts your brand at the intersection of frontier technology, world-class talent, and an unprecedented level of media attention for a student engineering team.

Elon Musk's repost of our competition win reached 1.1 million views. Your logo was on that machine.
